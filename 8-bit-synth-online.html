What makes the sound

Wario Synth plays MIDI files with three kinds of sound:

  • Pulse: bright, buzzy tones for leads and harmony.
  • Wave: wavetable tones for bass and other pitched parts.
  • Noise: short bursts for drums and percussion.

The engine maps the MIDI tracks to these sounds in your browser. It takes inspiration from the Game Boy, with extra voices for fuller arrangements.

FAQ

Can I play it with a MIDI keyboard?
No. This synth plays MIDI files; it has no live keyboard input or note editor.

Is it a Game Boy emulator?
No. It uses browser audio synthesis inspired by the Game Boy sound chip. It does not run Game Boy software.

Can I share the result?
Songs found through search can be shared by link. Uploaded MIDI files stay on your device; you can download a WAV clip instead.
