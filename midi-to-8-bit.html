How to convert MIDI to 8-bit

  1. Open Wario Synth and choose Upload MIDI. Select a .mid or .midi file up to 10 MB.
  2. Press Play 8-bit version. Use Preview original MIDI to compare.
  3. Choose Save 30s WAV to download up to 30 seconds from the start.

No file handy? Search for a song and choose a MIDI source instead. Local files are processed in your browser.

Convert MIDI to chiptune

MIDI holds notes and timing. Wario Synth reads those parts and plays them with pulse, wave and noise sounds. Higher parts can become a lead, lower parts a bass line, and percussion uses noise. Chords may become quick arpeggios.

The result depends on the transcription. If the melody gets lost, try another MIDI source or a simpler arrangement.

FAQ

Can I convert MP3 or WAV?
No. The converter needs MIDI note data. Search for a MIDI of the song, or export one from your music software.

Can I save the whole song?
Playback runs through the song. WAV downloads contain up to the first 30 seconds, plus a short release tail. MIDI export is not available.

Is it free?
Yes. No account is needed.
